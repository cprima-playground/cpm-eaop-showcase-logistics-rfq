Route-deviation approval is prohibited for the route agents. The accountable human decision remains visible.

What remains with the Transport Planner

  • Decide how recommendations fit customer commitments and priorities.
  • Validate whether a recommendation is realistic.
  • Monitor execution and communicate with carriers, drivers, customers, and colleagues.
  • Handle exceptions and accept, revise, or escalate a recommendation.

The agent is a bounded colleague for selected analytical and preparatory work—not a replacement for the complete job.

Central message

Business responsibility stays visible

Systems of record remain. Selected human responsibilities become agent capabilities. A2A connects those capabilities. MCP connects them to enterprise systems. Policy keeps every meaningful action governed.
